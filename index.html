Integrate the light.

Energy fraction = band irradiance ÷ total irradiance × 100%

Uses original ASTM G173-03 sampling points, linear interpolation at your band boundaries, and trapezoidal integration. The denominator is the integrated reference spectrum over 280–4000 nm, not a fixed 1000 W m⁻². AM1.5D uses the direct + circumsolar column.

Know what the number means.

This is the incident energy fraction, not a photon fraction, absorption efficiency, or photocatalytic efficiency. Narrow intervals are limited by the source data resolution. UV, visible, and IR preset boundaries are interface conventions.
